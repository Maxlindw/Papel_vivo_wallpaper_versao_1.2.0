Installation and first use

Start with a video you enjoy.

1. Install

Run Papel Vivo setup, select Português (Brasil) or English, read the terms and choose an application folder. The default destination is in your user folder.

2. Wait for completion

Choose shortcuts and follow the file extraction progress. Installation time varies by computer. If an error occurs, read the message and installation log before retrying.

3. Open and try

Open Papel Vivo, try the Brisa Azul sample or choose a local video, then select Preview. After downloading the complete package, setup and local video playback do not require internet access.

Your video. Your controls.

Test Preview before applying the video to your desktop.

Choose and apply

Use Choose video to open a local file. Preview plays inside the panel. Apply to desktop attempts to use the video as wallpaper. Check the result with Win+D.

Pause and restore

Pause / Resume controls playback. Restore original releases the player without closing the panel. Stop and exit closes the app and cancels any ongoing conversion.

FPS, sound and tray

30/60 FPS is a limit, not a guarantee. Zero volume means silent playback. When the tray is available, X hides the panel; use the tray menu to open or exit.

Português or English

Your language choice takes effect after restarting.

Choose in the panel

Open Language and select Português (Brasil) or English. The preference is saved on your computer.

Understand the confirmation

Yes restarts now. No applies the choice on the next launch without immediately changing the interface. Cancel keeps the previous preference.

After restarting

Playback stops during restart. Open the app and apply the video again if desired. Standard system dialog buttons may follow the Windows display language.

Compatibility and performance

Prefer a lightweight video your computer can play smoothly.

System and formats

Designed for 64-bit Windows 10/11. Supported containers include MP4/M4V, WebM, MKV, AVI and MOV, depending on available codecs. Start with 8-bit H.264, 720p or 1080p, at 30 FPS.

Larger videos

When width exceeds 1920 or height exceeds 1080, the app offers a lighter copy. Conversion runs locally, preserves the original and may consume CPU time and disk space.

Important limitations

Placement behind desktop icons depends on your Windows version and driver. Alternative mode may cover icons. Fixed FPS or resource use is not guaranteed. The screenshots were captured under Wine/Linux and do not validate the result on physical Windows.

Help, local data and removal

Quick guidance to keep using the app comfortably.

Slow, black or missing video

Try Brisa Azul, then an H.264 720p/1080p video at 30 FPS. Check the panel status. If wallpaper does not appear, restore the original and note your Windows version and what happened.

Report a problem

Open Diagnostics, select Copy report and review names and paths before sharing. Include the app version, Windows version, GPU/driver, video format and steps to reproduce. Reports are not sent automatically.

If Windows displays a warning

SmartScreen or an unknown-publisher warning may appear. Check the download source. If malware is detected, stop and investigate; do not disable protection to run the file.

Local files

Preferences, logs and converted copies are stored in %LOCALAPPDATA%\PapelVivo. Conversion does not overwrite the original videos.

Uninstall

Exit the app and use Windows Settings → Apps → Papel Vivo → Uninstall, or the Uninstall shortcut in the Start menu. Preferences, logs, converted copies and unrelated files are preserved.

Read without internet
